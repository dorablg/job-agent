⚡ One-click application filler

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar: ⚡ Fill application
  3. On a job, click Apply here. That copies the cover letter and opens the application page.
  4. On the application page, click ⚡ Fill application in your bookmarks bar. It fills your name, email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub and location, and pastes the cover letter if the page has a cover letter box.
  5. Upload your CV file, check everything, and submit it yourself.

It works on most forms (Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, many company sites). If a form is shown inside a box from another site, open that box's page directly, or use the copy buttons below.

Move your tracking to another device

Your statuses and notes are saved in this browser. To move them to your phone or another computer, export them here and import them there.
