⚡ One-click application filler

  1. Show your bookmarks bar: Ctrl+Shift+B.
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar: ⚡ Fill application
  3. On a job, open Apply kit and click 🚀 Apply. It copies the cover letter and opens the form.
  4. On the form, click ⚡ Fill application. It fills your name, email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub and location, and pastes the cover letter.
  5. Upload the ATS CV PDF for that job, answer the questions (copy them from the Apply kit), check everything and submit.

Works on most forms (Lever, Workable, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Teamtailor, many company sites). If a form is inside a box from another site, open that box's page directly.
